Privacy Policy — Drims Post Purchase

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Drims Post Purchase (“the App,” “we,” “us”) is a Shopify app that lets merchants show pre-purchase (cart) and post-purchase (after-checkout) upsell offers in their store. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, how long it is kept, and how to contact us.

Summary

  • The App stores merchant configuration and a snapshot of the products a merchant selects for their offers.
  • The App does not collect or store customer/buyer personal information (no names, emails, addresses, or payment details).
  • Data is used only to operate the App's upsell features.
  • We do not sell or share data for advertising.

What data we access and store

From the merchant (store owner), via the App's admin interface:

  • Offer and popup configuration you enter — headlines, descriptions, button text, colors, fonts, countdown settings, and enable/disable status.
  • The product/variant you select for an offer through Shopify's product picker. We store a snapshot of that product's public catalog details: product and variant IDs, title, image URL, price, and compare-at price. This is your store's own catalog data, not personal data.

Via Shopify APIs / the Shopify platform:

  • Your store's domain (e.g. your-store.myshopify.com) and an offline access token, which are stored to authenticate the App with your store. The App requests a single, read-only scope — read_products — used solely to power the product picker.

During checkout (transient, not stored):

  • When a buyer reaches the post-purchase step, Shopify calls the App with an order reference token and your store domain so the App can build the correct offer and sign the resulting order change. This request metadata is processed to generate the offer and is not used to identify any individual and is not retained as customer data.

What we do NOT collect:

  • We do not collect, store, or process buyer personal data — no customer names, email addresses, shipping/billing addresses, or payment/card information. Payments are handled entirely by Shopify.

How we use data

We use the data above solely to:

  • Authenticate the App with your Shopify store.
  • Save and display your configured pre-purchase and post-purchase offers.
  • Render the correct offer and apply the corresponding order change when a buyer accepts one.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers needed to run the App (see below).

Where data is processed (subprocessors)

The App runs on Render (application hosting) and stores configuration and session data in a Neon PostgreSQL database. These providers process data only to host and operate the App on our behalf. Data may be processed in the United States.

Data retention and deletion

  • Configuration and session data are retained while the App is installed on your store.
  • When you uninstall the App, or when Shopify sends a shop/redact request (approximately 48 hours after uninstall), the App deletes all data it holds for your store — offer configuration, popup configuration, and session records.
  • Because the App stores no customer personal data, customers/data_request and customers/redact requests are acknowledged but require no data to be returned or erased.

Compliance

The App honors Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact) and is designed to support merchants' obligations under privacy regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

For any questions or privacy requests, contact us at support@forpaws.store.
